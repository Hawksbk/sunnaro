Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Sunnaro ("the app") is a vitamin D reminder and tracker for Android, developed and published by the individual developer named on the app's Google Play page ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • Everything you enter (nicknames, photos, doses, dose history, settings) stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • No analytics, no tracking by us and no crash reporting. The app does not use your location.
  • The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show ads, as explained below. Nothing you enter in the app is ever given to the ad service.
  • Pro removes the ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the people you add (nickname, sticker or photo, dose, how often they take it, first dose day), which doses were taken and when, your reminder settings, your country, your language and unit choice, and whether you bought Pro. Dose information can be health-related, so we designed the app to keep it only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not collect it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you add a photo, the app copies it from the camera or photo picker into its private storage. Photos are never uploaded.

The home-screen widget and notifications show nicknames and stickers from this data. Notifications may be visible on your lock screen; you can change this in your phone's notification settings.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your personal device backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. We cannot access these backups.

You can delete everything at any time with "Delete all data" in the app's settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google explains how it uses this information here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are personalised. You can change them at any time under "Privacy choices (ads)" in the app's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google or privacy settings.
  • The app never gives AdMob your nicknames, photos, doses or any other information you enter.
  • With Pro, the app does not load ads at all.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that Pro was purchased, which it uses to unlock Pro and to restore it after a reinstall. We never see your card or payment details.

Official advice and links

The app contains summaries of official public-health advice with links to the source websites (for example national health services). Opening a link opens that website in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy. The app itself does not contact these websites.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you who still needs their dose.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state, advertising ID: used by Google AdMob in the free version.
  • Billing: used by Google Play for the Pro purchase.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or files. Photos are taken or picked through Android's own camera and photo picker.

Children

The app is meant for adults. Adults may add children as people to remind about vitamin D; this information stays on the device and is never shared. The app is not designed for use by children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) and the CCPA (California), you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. We do not collect or hold personal data about you: all app data stays on your device and is under your control. For data collected by Google for ads or purchases, see Google's privacy policy and settings. If you have a question about your data, write to us and we will answer.

Security

The app's data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Google Play and AdMob use encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change is significant, for example if the app starts collecting new information, we will point it out in the app or on its Google Play page before it takes effect.
